    How fees work
    Platform fee
    Free during the beta. After that, 0.50% of each new position, or 0.25% if your wallet holds 1,000,000 SZR or more. Closing a position has no platform fee.
    PancakeSwap fee
    0.25% of every swap, paid to the pool, not to us. It is already included in the amount you receive.
    Network fee
    A few cents in BNB for each transaction you sign. The first trade of a token also asks for an approval, which is one extra signature.
    Slippage
    The most the price may move between the quote and the trade. If it moves more, the trade is cancelled and you only pay the network fee.
    SZR tax
    Only applies when you buy or sell SZR itself. It changes with the active mode, shown in the SZR token section.

    Wallet Not connected

    Connecting shares your address only. It cannot move funds.
