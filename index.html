    Privacy & User Responsibility

    VEIL is intended to protect conversation content through end-to-end encryption. It does not guarantee absolute anonymity. A wallet address can become associated with a person through the way that wallet is used outside VEIL.

    IMPORTANT

    If you publish your wallet address or associate it with a name, username, email address, phone number, postal address, exchange account, social profile or other identifying information, that association is outside the control of VEIL and SCHIZORA.

    PRIVACY RECOMMENDATION

    For stronger separation between VEIL communications and your existing Web3 identity, consider a dedicated wallet that has not already been publicly associated with personal profiles or identity-linked services.
