01Summary

SCHIZORA ($SZR) is a BEP-20 token on BNB Chain with a fixed supply of 666,666,666 and seven tax modes that rotate every 24 hours. It is also the token behind the SCHIZORA website, a free toolkit for BNB Chain users with three parts: a token safety check, an automatic 7-day market outlook and non-custodial trading.

The idea is simple: before you buy a token, check it. The toolkit is free, needs no account and never holds user funds.

02The problem

New tokens launch on BNB Chain every day. Many are built to take money from buyers: they cannot be sold (honeypots), carry hidden or changeable taxes, let the owner mint new tokens or block wallets, keep most of the supply in a few wallets, or have liquidity that can be pulled at any moment.

Most buyers cannot read a smart contract, and the data needed to judge a token is spread across explorers, scanners and charts. SCHIZORA puts the key checks on one page, in plain words.

03The toolkit

1. Token Check

Paste any BNB Chain contract address. The site reads public on-chain and market data and runs seven checks, each explained in plain language:

  • Code: verified source, mint, blacklist, pause and proxy functions.
  • Exit: honeypot risk, buy and sell taxes, and whether taxes can be changed.
  • Owner: owner powers and how much the owner and creator hold.
  • Pool: liquidity depth, LP lock share and lock end date, and the price impact of a sale.
  • Holders: top holders, whales, and a dump test that estimates the price drop if the largest holder sells.
  • Crowd: buy and sell activity and volume.
  • Pulse: recent price movement.

The result is a safety score from 0 to 100. The score is an automated reading of public data. It does not guarantee that a token is safe.

2. 7-Day Outlook

An automatic outlook for more than 20 established coins (BNB, BTC, ETH, CAKE and others). Seven independent models (Trend, Cross, Pulse, Signal, Memory, Crowd and Contrarian) read the daily chart and each votes up, down or neutral. The site shows where the models agree, a likely 7-day price range, and how often each model was right in the past. These are signals, not recommendations.

3. Trade

Users connect their own wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Bitget Wallet and others) and swap through the PancakeSwap V2 router, paying with BNB or USDT. Before confirming, the site shows the token tax, the estimated price impact, the network fee and the user's stop-loss and take-profit levels. Built-in guards pause a buy when the PancakeSwap price differs from the market price by more than 2%, when the token symbol on-chain does not match, or when the price impact on SZR is above 25%.

Trading is non-custodial. Every transaction is signed in the user's own wallet. SCHIZORA never holds funds, seed phrases or private keys.

05The seven modes

The contract always runs in one of seven modes. Each mode sets its own buy and sell tax and its own split of the collected tax between the treasury, the liquidity wallet, the rewards wallet and the burn address.

Rotation

Once 24 hours have passed since the last switch, anyone can call rotateMode() on the contract, and the contract picks the next mode at random. An automated keeper calls it on schedule so the rotation does not depend on the team. The live mode, its taxes and the time of the next rotation are always shown on the website.

Parasite mode

Parasite is a temporary mode that only the designated watcher wallet can switch on, for at most 2 hours, when a tracked established token falls sharply. It is currently not automated. If it is ever automated, the rules will be published first: established tokens only, a fixed drop threshold and at most one activation per 24 hours.

06Supply and distribution

The total supply of 666,666,666 SZR was created at launch and cannot grow. The supply only shrinks, through the burn share of each tax.

  • Liquidity pool: SZR paired with BNB on PancakeSwap.
  • Burned: tokens sent to the dead address 0x…dEaD.
  • Tax wallets: treasury, liquidity and rewards wallets.
  • Presale contract: 166,666,666 SZR (25% of supply) sit in the presale contract 0x6DBc5a0EFC9848eB27B1c8b5C333af8DA956161A. The presale in May 2026 did not reach its target, all buyers were refunded, and these tokens have not moved since. They are not counted as circulating.
  • Founder and reserve wallets: the remaining supply is held by wallets controlled by the founder. These tokens are not counted as circulating.

The live split is read directly from the blockchain and shown in the SZR section of the website. The founder and reserve wallet addresses are published on request and in the listing applications, so anyone can verify them.

07Liquidity

Lock links and end dates will be published on the website.

08Revenue model

All tools are free during the beta. A small platform fee on trades made through the site is planned:

  • 0.50% per trade for regular users;
  • 0.25% for wallets holding at least 1,000,000 SZR.

The fee will be shown in the trade summary before each confirmation and can be checked on-chain. Token Check and the 7-Day Outlook stay free.

10Risks and transparency

  • Ownership is not renounced. The owner wallet keeps the administrative functions defined in the contract, such as setting the Parasite watcher and fee-exempt addresses. The source code can be read on BscScan.
  • Taxes change with the mode. The sell tax can be as high as 12% in Predator mode. Check the live mode before trading.
  • Concentrated supply. Most of the supply is held by founder wallets. A large sale from these wallets would move the price.
  • Low liquidity. With a small pool, even modest trades move the price strongly.
  • Automated analysis can be wrong. Token Check and the 7-Day Outlook read public data with fixed rules. They can miss risks and they cannot predict the future.
  • Smart contract risk. The contract has not been audited by a third party.
